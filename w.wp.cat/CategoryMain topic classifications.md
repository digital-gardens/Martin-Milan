This is a **[container category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category:Container_categories "Category:Container categories")**. Due to its scope, it should contain *only* [subcategories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Categorization#Subcategorization "Wikipedia:Categorization").

This is a list of Wikipedia's major topic classifications. They are used throughout Wikipedia to organize the presentation of links to articles in its various reference systems, including Wikipedia's [overviews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portal:Contents/Overviews "Portal:Contents/Overviews"), [outlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portal:Contents/Outlines "Portal:Contents/Outlines"), [glossaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portal:Contents/Glossaries "Portal:Contents/Glossaries"), [lists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portal:Contents/Lists "Portal:Contents/Lists"), [portals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portal:Contents/Portals "Portal:Contents/Portals"), [indices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portal:Contents/Indices "Portal:Contents/Indices"), and [categories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portal:Contents/Categories "Portal:Contents/Categories").

The top level of Wikipedia's overall [category system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Categorization "Wikipedia:Categorization"), including both articles and other project pages, is [Category:Contents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category:Contents "Category:Contents").

NOTE: The purpose of this category is to group major topic classifications in one place, for greater ease and for reference of users and editors of Wikipedia. This category is *not* intended to adhere to some strictly hierarchical structure.

Therefore, please do not remove entries from this category merely because they are subtopics of other entries here. Thank you.
