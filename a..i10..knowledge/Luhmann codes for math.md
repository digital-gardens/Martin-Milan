Yes, several prominent note-taking theorists, productivity experts, and software developers specifically discuss and implement collaborative Luhmann codes and shared Zettelkastens.


Bob Doto (Author and Note-Taking Theorist): He published a prominent framework titled How a Collaborative Zettelkasten Might Work: A Modest Proposal. Doto argues that in a multi-user Zettelkasten, team members should still create "main notes" independently to avoid consensus bottlenecks. The value of the collective knowledge base emerges naturally from the relationships and alphanumeric links created between those notes, rather than trying to co-write a "perfect" note together.
