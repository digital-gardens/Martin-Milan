"**We Will Rock You**" is a song by the British rock band [Queen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_\(band\) "Queen (band)") from their 1977 album *[News of the World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/News_of_the_World_\(album\) "News of the World (album)")*, written by guitarist [Brian May](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_May "Brian May").<sup about="#mwt20" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-3" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw="{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;ref&quot;,&quot;attrs&quot;:{},&quot;body&quot;:{&quot;id&quot;:&quot;mw-reference-text-cite_note-3&quot;}}"><a href="#cite_note-3" id="mwGQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwGg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwGw">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwHA">]</span></span></a></sup> *[Rolling Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone "Rolling Stone")* ranked it number 330 of "[The 500 Greatest Songs of All Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone's_500_Greatest_Songs_of_All_Time "Rolling Stone's 500 Greatest Songs of All Time")" in 2004,<sup about="#mwt23" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-4" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw="{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;ref&quot;,&quot;attrs&quot;:{},&quot;body&quot;:{&quot;id&quot;:&quot;mw-reference-text-cite_note-4&quot;}}"><a href="#cite_note-4" id="mwIA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwIQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwIg">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwIw">]</span></span></a></sup> and the [RIAA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RIAA "RIAA") it placed at number 146 on the [Songs of the Century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songs_of_the_Century "Songs of the Century") list in 2001. In 2009, "We Will Rock You" was inducted into the [Grammy Hall of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Hall_of_Fame_Award "Grammy Hall of Fame Award").<sup about="#mwt26" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-5" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw="{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;ref&quot;,&quot;attrs&quot;:{},&quot;body&quot;:{&quot;id&quot;:&quot;mw-reference-text-cite_note-5&quot;}}"><a href="#cite_note-5" id="mwJw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwKA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwKQ">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwKg">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt29" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-6" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw="{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;ref&quot;,&quot;attrs&quot;:{},&quot;body&quot;:{&quot;id&quot;:&quot;mw-reference-text-cite_note-6&quot;}}"><a href="#cite_note-6" id="mwKw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwLA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwLQ">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwLg">]</span></span></a></sup>
