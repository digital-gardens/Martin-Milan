## Less prompting, more doing.

## Create custom instructions.

To create a custom skill, simply describe what you want it to do in the prompt bar or in your skills page and Gemini will take care of the rest.

![Dark interface card for a beauty-maven skill, decorated with an orange 3D lipstick against a black background.](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/KVg_Qpxk_gVOFJT2288Lyi1YWvZF4tpQTrvOZlr1u4r2UUiGqZKuLJj8KiMnmXatxQjIb2yFCtN04EL_QAxmLdO5BDX1sDamgopm=e365-pa-nu-s0)

## Stack your skills.

You can combine multiple Gemini skills in a single prompt (for example, layering `/match-my-writing-style` and `/prep-for-meetings`) to complete complex, multi-step workflow automations seamlessly.

![Prompt buttons like "/photo-to-cad" surrounded by a pink piping bag, a green invoice envelope, and a blue wireframe high heel.](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/Kvblzhz-q_BtXS0_5wxlJRCWo5-R46dMH49gYTg6RHASIrrZ_XvT9ifcjhOfNyJswSmk5eaWNyUAxQhGpCj0ufvO9-evrisuO_y0ug=e365-pa-nu-s0)

## Give more context.

Add files when uploading a skill.md file to add more context that Gemini can reference each time you use the skill.

## Frequently asked questions

Gems and skills are used for similar purposes to customize your Gemini experience for things you do time and again. Later this year, we’re transitioning from gems to skills to give you an even better, more flexible way to customize Gemini. skills will let you do more, like using them right in your current chat without having to switch tabs or stacking multiple skills together, to create even more customized workflows.

[

Learn more about the Gems to skills migration

](https://support.google.com/gemini?p=gems_to_skills&hl=en-US)

.

As we bring more functionality to skills, we will remove support for Gems starting in November for individuals with personal accounts, March 2027 for Workspace business, enterprise, and nonprofit customers, and June 2027 for Workspace education customers.To make this transition seamless, we will automatically migrate your Gems into skills when Gems go away. You can learn more about how to transition your Gems into skills by visiting our [help center](https://support.google.com/gemini?p=gems_to_skills&hl=en-US).

You can create a skill in two easy ways: simply ask Gemini in regular chat (e.g., "Create a skill that formats my weekly status reports"), or build, upload, or customize skills directly in the skills Manager in settings.

Yes, you can upload skill.md files directly to Gemini to instantly import the skill. To upload, go to Settings > skills > Upload.
