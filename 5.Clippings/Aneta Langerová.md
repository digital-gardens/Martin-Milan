**Aneta Langerová** (born 26 November 1986) is a [Czech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_people "Czech people") pop singer. Born in [Benešov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bene%C5%A1ov "Benešov"), Czech Republic and grew up in [Říčany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%98%C3%AD%C4%8Dany "Říčany"), she first rose to fame at age 17 as the first winner of *[Česko hledá SuperStar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SuperStar_\(Czech_and_Slovak_TV_series\) "SuperStar (Czech and Slovak TV series)")*,[^1] the Czech version of *[Pop Idol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_Idol "Pop Idol")*, in June 2004. Later that year she released her first album, *Spousta andělů*, which became a long-term bestseller in the Czech Republic.[^2] She has since released two further albums and is currently one of the most popular musical artists in the [Czech Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Republic "Czech Republic").

In 2005 and 2006, she ranked first in the category 'Best Female Singer' in the Czech music poll [Český slavík](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%8Cesk%C3%BD_slav%C3%ADk "Český slavík").[^3] [^4] In 2004, 2007 and 2012 she placed first in the same category of the [Anděl Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/And%C4%9Bl_Awards "Anděl Awards"), organized by the *Czech Academy of Popular Music*.[^5]

She finished in second place in the Czech version of *[Dancing with the Stars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars "Dancing with the Stars")* - *[StarDance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/StarDance_\(Czech_TV_series\)_season_4 "StarDance (Czech TV series) season 4")* in 2010.

## Biography

Langerová grew up in [Říčany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%98%C3%AD%C4%8Dany "Říčany"), a town in the Central Bohemian Region.[^6] At age six, she began studying piano, and at age eight, she began taking guitar classes and singing in a choir with a local choir instructor. Langerová has credited her mother, who played piano and guitar around her house when she was growing up, with nurturing her musical talent.

In her teenage years, Langerová became more serious about music. At age 14, Langerová joined a local band called SPB in [Sázava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A1zava_\(Bene%C5%A1ov_District\) "Sázava (Benešov District)") [^7] and then participated in and won several small TV talent contests (the Doremi TV competition and the Star Starters (in Czech: *Rozjezdy pro hvězdy*) contest) before participating in the first season of *Česko hledá SuperStar,* a popular Czech television show based on the *Pop Idol* format.

Langerová won the *Česko hledá SuperStar* competition with a 79 percent vote share in June 2004.[^8] In the course of the next three months, she quickly recorded her first album, *Spousta andělů* (Many Angels), composed of songs written by professional pop songwriters. The album enjoyed chart success in the Czech Republic, remaining at the top for 11 weeks in 2005.[^9] In 2005 and 2006, Langerová toured, released a live album and DVD and was closely involved with the Světluška Foundation, a charitable organization that works with blind children.[^10] Langerová played multiple benefit concerts for the organization and released a single, "Píseň pro světlušku" (Song for Světluška, "firefly" in Czech), to raise money for the group.

In 2007, Langerová released her second studio album, *Dotyk* (Contact), which showed her abilities beyond singing: she wrote or co-wrote all of the songs on the album and played guitar, piano, and bass on several tracks.

In 2017, she starred in a [Czech Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Television "Czech Television") film titled *8 hlav šílenství* (*8 Heads of Madness*), portraying Soviet poet and gulag survivor [Anna Barkova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Barkova "Anna Barkova"). The film is mainly about her life in the camps and the women she loved.[^11]

## Personal life

Langerová is openly [lesbian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lesbian "Lesbian").[^12]

#### Dotyk

Released 21 May 2007, *Dotyk* is a departure from Langerová's previous efforts, dismissed by many as the product of slick production and professional songwriters. On "Dotyk," Langerová, had a hand in writing the music on all ten tracks (often sharing writing credits with her producers and other players on the album), and wrote four of the songs by herself. Langerová plays guitar, bass, and piano on a handful of tracks. "Dotyk" was produced by [Jan P. Muchow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan_P._Muchow "Jan P. Muchow"), best known as a film composer and half of the Czech experimental rock group [Ecstasy of Saint Theresa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecstasy_of_Saint_Theresa_\(band\) "Ecstasy of Saint Theresa (band)").

"Dotyk" translates to English as "Contact" or, alternatively, as "Touch."
